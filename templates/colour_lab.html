STUDENT MODE

Colour thinking

01
Identify the canvas

Virgin vs artificial colour makes a big difference. Record level, visible tone, history and condition.

02
Define the destination

Choose target level and tone separately. “Honey caramel” is a visual goal; the route depends on the starting canvas.

03
Plan the correction

Decide whether the service needs lift, deposit, neutralisation, pre-lightening or a combination.

04
Verify the brand system

Use the selected brand's own shade chart, developer rules and processing instructions. Record the tested formula.

05
Learn from the result

Photograph the outcome and write what you would keep or change next time.
